    Sync favourites

    Pull your favourites from your EMF account into this browser. While a token is stored, the favourites here mirror your EMF account: syncing replaces them, and the stars become read-only. To change one, favourite it on emfcamp.org and sync, or Forget token to manage favourites here instead.

    Get your feed link
    Go to emfcamp.org/favourites and log in. Once logged in, tap on the json feed link, copy the link URL and paste it here. It is stored in this browser and sent to EMF through this site's own relay. If you don't want to do this, then you can also paste the JSON instead below.

    No token? Paste the JSON instead

    While logged in, open your favourites JSON, select all, copy, and paste it below.
